# Power-BI-Sales-Dashboard-Project
## Project overview

This Power BI project turns retail sales data into an interactive performance dashboard for managers and analysts. It provides a single view of sales, profit, order quantity, shipping time, regional contribution, customer segments, payment behavior, product categories, and state-level activity.

The dashboard is designed to answer three practical questions:

- Where is revenue coming from?
- Which customer and product groups drive performance?
- When and where should the business focus next?
## Dashboard highlights

| KPI | Dashboard result |
|---|---:|
| Total sales | 1.6M |
| Total profit | 175K |
| Quantity sold | 22K |
| Average ship days | 4 |
| Approximate profit margin | 10.9% |

> The margin is calculated from the displayed dashboard totals: 175K ÷ 1.6M. Figures are rounded as shown in the dashboard.
## Key business insights

1. **The West is the largest sales region.** It contributes 33% of sales, followed by East at 29%, Central at 22%, and South at 16%. West and East together represent 62% of total sales.
2. **Consumer customers are the core segment.** Consumer contributes 48% of sales, compared with Corporate at 33% and Home Office at 19%.
3. **Office Supplies leads category revenue.** It generates 0.64M, ahead of Technology at 0.47M and Furniture at 0.45M.
4. **Phones are the top displayed sub-category.** Phones contribute 0.20M, followed by Chairs at 0.18M and Binders at 0.17M.
5. **Standard Class dominates shipping.** It accounts for 0.33M among the displayed ship modes, substantially more than Second Class (0.11M), First Class (0.08M), and Same Day (0.03M).
6. **COD is the most-used payment mode.** Cash on Delivery represents 43%, followed by Online at 35% and Cards at 22%.
7. **Year-end momentum is strong.** The monthly sales and profit views show the highest levels late in the year, with November and December standing out for sales and December for profit.
## Recommended actions

- Protect the West and East regions while investigating the South region's lower 16% contribution.
- Prioritize Consumer-focused campaigns because this segment generates nearly half of sales.
- Use Office Supplies as a volume anchor and cross-sell higher-value Technology products.
- Prepare inventory and marketing capacity ahead of the late-year sales increase.
- Review the profitability and delivery cost of Standard Class because it carries the largest displayed shipping volume.
- Encourage prepaid digital payments where appropriate to reduce dependence on COD.

## Dashboard features

- KPI cards for sales, profit, quantity, and shipping time
- Region slicer for interactive filtering
- Year-over-year monthly sales and profit trends
- Regional, segment, and payment-mode contribution analysis
- State-level geographic view
- Category, sub-category, and shipping-mode comparisons

## Tools and skills demonstrated

- **Power BI:** dashboard design and interactive reporting
- **Power Query:** data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX:** KPI and analytical measure creation
- **Data visualization:** chart selection, layout, and visual hierarchy
- **Business analysis:** translating dashboard results into decisions
- **Data storytelling:** presenting findings for managers and recruiters

## Portfolio summary

Built an interactive Power BI sales dashboard that revealed the West as the leading region (33%), Consumer as the largest segment (48%), and Office Supplies as the top category (0.64M). The analysis also identified strong year-end sales momentum and opportunities to improve performance in the South region, optimize shipping mix, and reduce COD dependence.
